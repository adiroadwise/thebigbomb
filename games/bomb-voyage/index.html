🪺 Bomb Voyage

Fly the stork. Land the bomb gently. Don't miss the pad.

Click anywhere to flap. Click left/right of the stork to nudge it that way — only a little. Quick successive clicks build a combo for a bigger flap/nudge.

Gravity never lets up. Flap to slow your fall, but every click also nudges you left or right depending on which side of the stork you click — overcorrect and you'll drift off target. Click twice or three times in quick succession for a bigger flap and nudge (×1.5, ×2.0). The only safe spot is the little woven cradle in the middle of the pad — land there gently and it's a catch; anywhere else, on the pad or off it, means BOOM, Looney-Tunes style: soot, stars, and a very dazed stork. Every successful landing levels you up and adds one more hazard: a tree, then a building, then a crop-sprayer plane drifting across the sky, then a barn that swallows the cradle under its roof — you'll have to duck in through its open side at low altitude to reach it. Crash and it's back to level 1.
