Hover a bomb group to preview its chain and score.

Only bombs of the same color that touch (up/down/left/right) can explode together — click a group of 2 or more to detonate it. Removed bombs fall down and fresh bombs keep dropping in from the top, so the board never empties and the game never ends. Bigger groups score more, and the board gets harder (more colors) the higher your score climbs. There's always at least one match available — go for a new best score!
